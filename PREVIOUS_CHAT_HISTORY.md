### 🏛️ **Project Overview: CivicPulse**
**CivicPulse** is an AI-powered civic issue reporting, resolution, and accountability platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and municipal authorities (using municipal models such as Greater Chennai Corporation / urban local bodies as the reference).

---

### 🔑 **Key Architecture & Capabilities Understood**

1. **Multimodal & Multilingual Citizen Intake**:
   - **Reporting Channels**: Photo capture, Voice recording (Speech-to-Text via **Sarvam AI**), Text description, and GPS coordinates/map pin.
   - **Language Support**: Local Indian languages (Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, and English) processed and translated via Sarvam AI.
   - **Offline Capability**: Local queue storage with automatic background synchronization when internet connectivity resumes.

2. **AI Processing Pipeline**:
   - **Auto-Categorization & Severity**: Classifies civic issues (potholes, garbage dumps, storm water drain clogs, streetlights, sanitation, water leakage, etc.) and assigns severity levels (Low, Medium, High, Emergency) with SLA timelines.
   - **Intelligent Deduplication & Clustering**: Detects duplicate reports using spatial distance (GPS < 50–100m), text/embedding similarity, and image comparison, grouping them into single parent issues with citizen upvotes/subscribers.
   - **Spam & Abuse Protection**: Image integrity verification and text moderation before routing.

3. **Department Routing & Escalation Hierarchy**:
   - **Automatic Department Routing**: Routes complaints to the responsible wing (Solid Waste Management, Electrical/Lighting, Storm Water Drains, Roads & Footpaths, Health & Sanitation, Water Supply & Sewerage, Parks, Revenue/Encroachments).
   - **Hierarchical SLA Escalation**: If lower-level officers (e.g., Assistant Engineers) fail to act within the SLA, complaints auto-escalate up the chain (AEE $\rightarrow$ Executive Engineer $\rightarrow$ Zonal Officer $\rightarrow$ Deputy Commissioner $\rightarrow$ Commissioner).

4. **Accountability & Closed-Loop Verification**:
   - **Officer Dashboard**: Field survey notes, fund allocation flags, work order tracking, and mandatory geo-tagged **Before/After** proof photos.
   - **Citizen Verification Loop**: Citizens can verify the fix and either **Confirm Resolution** or **Reopen** the ticket within a feedback window if work is incomplete.

5. **Safety & Identity Verification (Hackathon Mode)**:
   - **Demo/Mock Identity System**: Prevents multiple fake accounts and spam while adhering to privacy principles. Stored separately using synthetic 12-digit demo references without storing or connecting to real UIDAI systems.
